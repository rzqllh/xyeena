**Questions/required fields:** What organizational problem exists; baseline and source; who owns the outcome; impact of doing nothing; how will change be measured?

## Stakeholders and responsibilities

**Questions/required fields:** Sponsor, process owner, operator, beneficiary, external actor; approval boundary and escalation path.

- `[[FILL: project-specific decision, rationale and direct reference]]`

## Business capabilities and rules

**Questions/required fields:** Cost ceiling, time horizon, staffing, dependencies, customer-impact assumptions; do not invent revenue.

## Boundaries and governance

**Questions/required fields:** In-scope operations, explicitly excluded work, policy approvals and conditions that would reopen decisions.

**Questions/required fields:** Observable metrics, measurement source, business sign-off, links to PRD requirements.
